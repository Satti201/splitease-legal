Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the SplitEase Help Center. Below are answers to common questions about using SplitEase for hostel room budgeting and shared expenses.

1. How do I add room mates to my group?

Every group generated in SplitEase receives a unique 6-character invite code (e.g. X7K9P2). Share this code with your room mates. They can tap Enter Code on their app dashboard to join instantly.

2. How does SplitEase calculate debt minimization?

Our algorithm analyzes all expenses entered in a group and automatically cancels out cross-debts. Instead of making 10 separate payments to each other, SplitEase calculates the minimum direct payments required to settle all group debts.

3. How do I send WhatsApp debt reminders?

Inside any group, switch to the Balances tab or tap Settle Up. Tap the Send WhatsApp Reminder button next to a debtor to generate a pre-filled reminder message on WhatsApp.

4. Can I split bills unequally?

Yes. When adding an expense, choose between Equal, Percentage, or Exact Amount splitting options.
