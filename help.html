Help & FAQ

Find answers to the most common questions about SplitEase.

Getting Started
What is SplitEase? ▾
SplitEase is an expense splitting app for groups — roommates, hostel rooms, trips, or any shared living situation. Add expenses, see who owes what, and settle up with the minimum number of payments.
How do I create a group? ▾
On the main screen, tap New group. Give it a name, choose a type (hostel, trip, home etc.) and select your currency. The group is created instantly and you get a 6-character invite code to share with your roommates.
How do others join my group? ▾
Share the 6-character invite code with your roommates. They tap Enter code on the main screen, type the code, and they're in. You can copy the code from the group card on the home screen.
Expenses
How do I add an expense? ▾
Open the group, tap + Add expense. Enter the amount, what it was for, who paid, which members to split with, and the category. The app calculates each person's share automatically and saves it in real time.
How is the expense split? ▾
Expenses are split equally between all selected members by default. For example, if 3 people split a PKR 3000 expense, each person owes PKR 1000. You can uncheck members to exclude them from a specific expense.
Can I delete an expense? ▾
Yes, but only the person who added the expense can delete it. Swipe left on the expense card in the Expenses tab to reveal the delete button. Other members will see the expense but cannot delete it.
Settlements & Balances
How does the settlement calculation work? ▾
SplitEase uses a minimum transactions algorithm. It calculates each person's net balance (total paid minus fair share), then figures out the fewest payments needed to settle all debts. For example, 5 people need at most 4 payments — not 10.
What does "You owe" vs "You get back" mean? ▾
You owe means you have paid less than your fair share across all group expenses. You get back means you have paid more than your fair share and others need to pay you.
How do I mark a payment as paid? ▾
Go to the group → tap Settle up. You will see the payments you need to make. Once you have physically paid someone (cash, bank transfer, etc.), tap I've paid this and confirm. The payment disappears from the list for everyone in real time.
Can I share the settlement summary? ▾
Yes. On the Settle up screen, tap the Share settlement summary button. This generates a clean text summary that you can share to WhatsApp, SMS, or any messaging app.
Account
How do I change my display name? ▾
Tap your avatar icon on the main screen → tap your name under Display name → tap Edit → enter your new name and tap Save.
How do I delete my account? ▾
Go to your profile → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete account. This will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days. Note: your expenses in shared groups will remain visible to other members.
I signed in with Google but can't see my groups. ▾
Make sure you are signed in with the same Google account you used to create or join the groups. Groups are tied to your account UID, not just your email. Sign out and sign back in with the correct account.
