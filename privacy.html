Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

SplitEase ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, ever.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use SplitEase:

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you register or sign in with Google.
  • Expense data: Expense titles, amounts, dates, categories, and who paid — exactly what you enter in the app.
  • Group data: Group names, types, members, and invite codes.
  • Device token: A Firebase Cloud Messaging token to send you push notifications.
  • Usage data: Basic app usage analytics collected anonymously by Firebase.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide the expense splitting and settlement service
  • To send push notifications when group members add expenses or mark payments
  • To sync data in real time across your devices and group members
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure with enterprise-grade security. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

Access to your group data is restricted to group members only. Our Firestore security rules ensure no one outside your group can read your expenses or settlement data.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database (Firestore), push notifications (FCM), and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method. Google Privacy Policy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to outside parties except as described in this policy. Your expense data is shared only with members of the same group — people you have explicitly invited or joined with.

Push Notifications

SplitEase sends push notifications to inform group members when:

  • A new expense is added to a group you're in
  • A payment owed to you is marked as paid

You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data inside the app at any time.
  • Correction: You can update your display name in the Profile screen.
  • Deletion: You can delete your account from Profile → Delete account. Your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Portability: Contact us to request an export of your data.

Children's Privacy

SplitEase is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
