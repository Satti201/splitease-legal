Privacy Policy for SplitEase

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

SplitEase ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Display Name, Email Address, and User ID (UID) provided during registration or Google Sign-In.
  • Group Expense Data: Group names, member lists, expense titles, amounts, split ratios, and payment history entered into the app.
  • Device Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push notification tokens to notify you about group settlements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate group balances and debt minimization settlements.
  • To synchronize real-time group expense data across members.
  • To deliver push notifications for debt reminders.

3. Data Storage & Third-Party Services

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase Firestore and protected with industry-standard encryption protocols. We do not sell or trade your personal data to third parties.
