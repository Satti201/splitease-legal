Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety policies, SplitEase users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and associated data.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the SplitEase app on your device.
  2. Go to Profile & Settings (tap your profile icon in top right).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt to permanently wipe your profile and Firestore records.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request via Email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

Send an email from your registered address to lightningdragon116@gmail.com with the subject line SplitEase Account Deletion Request.

Data Deletion & Retention Scope

Upon account deletion, your user profile record (Name, Email, UID) in Google Firebase Firestore and your Firebase Authentication profile will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
